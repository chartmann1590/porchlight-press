# Content & Attribution Policy

Porchlight Press exists to point readers **toward** real reporting, not to replace it. This page explains how we handle other people's work and how our AI newsroom behaves.

## What we collect from news sources

- Only from **public feeds** (RSS/Atom), **official government and public-agency sources**, and **public-domain** material.
- From news publishers we use only the **headline, link, publication time, and the short summary the publisher includes in its own feed**, and only as far as each source allows.
- **We never copy or republish full articles**, and we never get around paywalls, logins, or `robots.txt` rules.
- Every source is listed in a public registry in this repository, along with the rules for how each one may be used.

## How stories are credited

- Every story shows **each news organization it's based on**, the original headline, when it was published, and a **Read original** link.
- AI-written briefs are **always** labelled **AI Newsroom** and are never presented as a publisher's own work.
- When an AI brief doesn't pass our checks, we show the **original headline and link**, and nothing we wrote.

## Photos

- We **don't reuse publishers' photos** unless the publisher's license explicitly allows it.
- Other photos come from **public-domain government sources** or **Wikimedia Commons** under open licenses (such as CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA). Every one carries its credit and license line.
- A photo that shows a place or organization in the story, rather than the event itself, is captioned **"File photo"**. We never pass off an unrelated photo as a picture of the news.

## AI newsroom rules

The AI is instructed and checked to:

- use **only** facts reported by the listed sources;
- **never invent** names, numbers, dates, places, quotes, or sources;
- stay **neutral**: no opinions, no endorsements, no voting advice, and no ranking of parties or candidates;
- **attribute disagreements** ("officials said 300 were affected; the utility said 500");
- avoid long passages copied word-for-word from any source.

Any brief that fails an automatic check is thrown out and replaced with a plain source card.

## Translation and read-aloud

Translations and spoken audio are generated **on the reader's phone** for their personal use. They're labelled as machine translations, and the original is always available.

## Corrections and takedowns

- **Readers:** use **Report a problem with this story** in the app, or email [me@charleshartman.com](mailto:me@charleshartman.com).
- **Publishers and rights holders:** if you'd like your content credited differently, used less, or removed entirely, email [me@charleshartman.com](mailto:me@charleshartman.com) with the story link or your feed URL. We'll act within **5 business days**, and a publisher's request to stop using its feed is always honored.

## What we won't publish

Full copyrighted articles, content from sources that don't allow reuse, unverified rumors from single anonymous sources, and material that is hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, or that promotes violence.
