# Security Policy

Thanks for helping keep Porchlight Press and its readers safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't report security problems in public issues.** Use one of these private channels instead:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting (preferred):** go to the [Security tab](https://github.com/chartmann1590/porchlight-press/security) and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
2. **Email:** [me@charleshartman.com](mailto:me@charleshartman.com) with the subject line **"Security: Porchlight Press"**.

Please include:

- what the problem is and where it is (app version, file, URL, or feature);
- steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept;
- what an attacker could do with it;
- how you'd like to be credited, if at all.

## What to expect

| Step | Target time |
|---|---|
| We confirm we got your report | within 3 business days |
| First assessment (is it valid, and how serious) | within 10 business days |
| Fix released for serious issues | as quickly as possible, usually within 30 days |

We'll keep you updated. With your permission, we'll credit you when the fix is released.

## Scope

**In scope**

- The Porchlight Press Android app
- The news pipeline and GitHub Actions workflows in this repository
- The published news files and story pages at `chartmann1590.github.io/porchlight-press`

**Out of scope**

- Third-party services we use (Google AdMob, Firebase, ML Kit, Google Play, GitHub, the National Weather Service, MET Norway, Wikimedia Commons, news publishers). Please report those to the service directly.
- Problems that need a rooted or already-compromised device
- Automated scanner output without a demonstrated impact
- Missing security headers on static files with no demonstrated exploit
- Denial-of-service or load testing. **Please don't do this.**

## Safe harbor

If you act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, don't access or change other people's data, and give us reasonable time to fix the problem before telling anyone else, we won't take legal action against you for your research.

## Supported versions

Only the **latest version** of the app on Google Play gets security fixes. Please update before reporting.
