Porchlight Press is your hometown newspaper, rebuilt for your phone. Free to download and free to use — no account, no paywall.

A NEWSPAPER, NOT A FEED
A front page with a masthead, headlines, and sections, in classic newsprint or clean modern style, light or dark. Local, region, state, nation, and world — built around the town you pick.

WEATHER BUILT IN
Today's conditions, the forecast, and severe-weather alerts (U.S. National Weather Service; MET Norway outside the US).

EVERY STORY SHOWS ITS SOURCES
Short, neutral briefs list every outlet they're based on, with a link to the original reporting. We send readers to local journalists; we don't replace them.

HONEST AI
Briefs are written on free public infrastructure and clearly labelled as AI-written. Automatic checks reject any brief that adds facts the sources didn't report — then you see the original headline and link instead. Always check the sources for anything important.

READ YOUR WAY
Dozens of languages (translated on your phone), Listen to any story, download today's paper as a PDF, share via Android, read offline, save and search, and control notifications (severe weather, breaking local, morning/evening editions, quiet hours).

FREE, WITH A FEW ADS
Clearly labelled Advertisement slots between sections — never inside stories, never pop-ups. Personalized only with your consent (change anytime in Settings → Privacy options).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. Your places, reading, saves, and searches stay on your phone. Location is coarse-only and optional (ZIP or town picker works). Crash reports and usage stats are off unless you turn them on.
