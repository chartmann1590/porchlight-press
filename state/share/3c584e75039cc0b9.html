New law mandates cosmetology training for all hair types

A new state law requires cosmetology programs to teach students how to handle various hair textures and types, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC. The law was implemented to accommodate the diversity of hair among students at Albany High School, where students receive hands-on training through a free weekly clinic.
