Dry conditions in Albany County increase wildfire risk

Dry weather conditions in Albany County have raised concerns about wildfires, according to WTEN News10. The Cobleskill Fire Department Lieutenant, Brian Head, noted that fires are common this time of year as seasons change. The dry conditions, with no rain in over two weeks, have increased the risk of brush and wildfires in the area.
