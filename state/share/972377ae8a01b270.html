New York expands free SUNY and CUNY tuition to those with college degrees

Governor Kathy Hochul announced on Monday that New York's free community college program will expand in January 2027 to include adults aged 25 to 55 who already hold degrees but wish to retrain in high-demand fields affected by artificial intelligence.
