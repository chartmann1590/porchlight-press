Black travelers on Route 66 weren't welcome at most gas stations — except this one

Threatt Filling Station in Luther, Okla., was the first Black-owned gas station on Route 66. At a time when Black families needed the Green Book to find places to stay, eat, use the bathroom, it provided fuel and respite.
