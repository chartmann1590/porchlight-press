Troy City Council approves law limiting license plate reader data retention

Troy City Council approved a law limiting the retention of data from automated license plate readers, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The measure was passed after the council overrode Mayor Carmella Mantello's veto on legislation that restricts Flock Safety camera data storage to 72 hours, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC.
