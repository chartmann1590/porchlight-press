Guilderland pauses data centers for a year

Guilderland, N.Y., has paused new data centers for a year amid state moratorium.

Guilderland, N.Y., has paused new data centers for a year amid a state-wide moratorium, according to WTEN News10. The pause follows a one-year statewide ban on certain large data centers, as reported by the source. The decision applies to projects at 50 megawatts or more, but Guilderland has taken further action to ban all data centers in the area.
