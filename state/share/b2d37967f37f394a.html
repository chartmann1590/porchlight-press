Offensive woes sink Siena women's basketball against Army

Siena Women's Basketball (2-4) battled Army West Point (5-1) throughout three quarters Thursday evening at UHY Center, but the Black Knights pulled away in the fourth to secure a 54-41 victory. The Saints were held to 14-for-44 shooting from the field and committed 24 turnovers.
