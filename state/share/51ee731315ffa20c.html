Ceiling fan causes fire at Clifton Park group home

On Sunday, the Vischer Ferry Volunteer Fire Company responded to a fire at the Capital District Developmental Disabilities Services Office/Office of People with Developmental Disabilities group home in Clifton Park. Fire Officials have since determined the fire was deemed accidental.
