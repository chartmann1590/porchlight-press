Yerdon gets 21 years in prison for having child sex abuse images

James Yerdon, a man accused of sexually abusing children in Albany County and admitted to receiving and possessing child sex abuse images has been sentenced in federal court. According to the U.S. Attorney's Office, Yerdon was sentenced to 21 years and 10 months in prison.
