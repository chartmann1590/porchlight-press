Harrison's OT winner lifts Schuylerville to Class B title

With the Section 2, Class B boys soccer championship on the line, Schuylerville's Colden Harrison connected on a header that found the back of the net in overtime to secure the second-ever section title for the Black Horses, who got the better of Broadalbin-Perth in a 1-0 battle.
