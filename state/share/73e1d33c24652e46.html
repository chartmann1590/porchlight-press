Three southern states have filed legal challenges against New York's shield laws

Three southern states have filed legal challenges against New York's shield laws, according to WTEN News10 ABC. The states of Louisiana, Alabama, and Arkansas are seeking to invalidate the laws that protect doctors who send abortion medication to patients in states with restrictive regulations.
