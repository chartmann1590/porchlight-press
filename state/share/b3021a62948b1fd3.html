Haines Falls Receives State Funding for Pedestrian Safety

Haines Falls gets state funding to enhance pedestrian accessibility.

Greene County was given more than $730,000 in state funds to enhance pedestrian accessibility in Haines Falls, as reported by WTEN News10. The funds will be used to construct an ADA-compliant sidewalk along State Route 23A and North Lake Road. The Hunter Area Trail Committee confirmed the funding allocation.
