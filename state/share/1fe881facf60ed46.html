Wildfire in Rensselaer County closes trails and prompts state response

The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation reported that crews are addressing an active wildfire in Rensselaer County. Trails in the area are closed, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The fire occurs during dry weather and follows a wildfire that burned 250 acres in Rensselaer County and western Massachusetts a week ago.
