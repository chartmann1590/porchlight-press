Teenager reported missing out of Dutchess County found in good health

On Sunday, the New York State Police requested the public's help in locating a 14-year-old boy who was reported missing out of the town of Wappinger in Dutchess County. The teen was last seen leaving his home on Saturday, but has since been found in good health, according to authorities.
