Saratoga County Restaurants Offer Prix Fixe Menus

Saratoga County's restaurant week features prix fixe menus for $15 to $45.

Saratoga County will host its 21st annual restaurant week from November 2 to 8. During the event, restaurants will offer prix fixe menus priced at $15, $25, $35, or $45, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC.
