Audit raises concerns over ARPA funds in Albany initiative

Audit of Albany's 'Albany for All' initiative reveals mismanagement of ARPA funds

An audit of the city's 'Albany for All' initiative found issues with ARPA funds, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio and WTEN News10. The $25 million program, designed to aid the city's post-pandemic recovery, was found to have mishandled federal pandemic relief funds, WAMC Northeast Public Radio reported. The audit also showed that the city's final two budgets included federal pandemic relief funds for the initiative, which were depleted by the end of 2024, WAMC Northeast Public Radio said.
