Albany Common Council passes commuter tax proposal

Albany Common Council has passed a commuter tax proposal, which would deduct 0.3% from non-resident paychecks, according to WTEN News10. The tax is being considered by some Albany Common Council members, but faces steep odds in the New York State Legislature, as reported by WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The proposal is under debate in the New York State Legislature, with state lawmakers and labor unions opposing the plan, according to WTEN News10.
