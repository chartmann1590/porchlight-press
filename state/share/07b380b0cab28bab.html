Albany Common Council passes commuter tax proposal

Albany Common Council has passed a commuter tax proposal, according to WTEN News10 ABC. The tax would deduct 0.3% from non-resident paychecks, as reported. The proposal faces opposition from state lawmakers and labor unions, as noted in the sources. The tax is being considered by some Albany Common Council members, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio.
