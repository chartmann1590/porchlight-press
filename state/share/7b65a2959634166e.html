Breast cancer awareness advocate seeks to teach about risk to all people

The American Cancer Society says a man's average lifetime risk of getting breast cancer is 1 in 755. Jake Messier is among the fewer than 1% of American men who find themselves as that one. The social media influencer and advocate has Stage 4 metastatic breast cancer. He was first diagnosed in 2023.
