Holiday train returns to Capital Region

Canadian train benefits local food banks and makes stops in Capital Region towns

The Canadian Pacific Holiday Train (CPKC) is returning to the Capital Region, according to WTEN News10. The train makes stops in Menands, Mechanicville, and Fort Edward. It benefits local food banks and brings entertainment along the way. The train is returning to the Capital Region from Canada.
