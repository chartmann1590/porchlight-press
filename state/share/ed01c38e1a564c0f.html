Albany Audit Finds Budget Gaps in Community Initiative

Audit of Albany for All initiative reveals financial shortfalls and oversight issues

Chief City Auditor Sam Fein released an audit of the Albany for All initiative, which was funded with $25 million from ARPA. The audit identified multimillion-dollar budget shortfalls and oversight failures, according to the City of Albany News Flash. The initiative is part of a community investment program in Albany, New York.
