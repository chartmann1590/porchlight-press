Saratoga Springs Tax Increase Proposal to Tackle Budget Shortfall

Saratoga Springs officials plan 18% tax hike to counter $7 million deficit by 2027.

Saratoga Springs city officials have suggested raising taxes by 18% in the coming year to manage a budget shortfall that could reach $7 million by 2027, as reported by WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The proposal follows challenges in budget management across the Capital Region.
