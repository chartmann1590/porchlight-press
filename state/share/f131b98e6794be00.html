Shen and Guilderland advance to girls soccer section final

Two girls soccer teams advance to the section final after winning their respective semifinals.

Shenendehowa and Guilderland advanced to the 2026 Class AAA, Section II final after winning their semifinals, according to WTEN News10. The teams will face off in a rematch of their semis matchup from a year ago. The Dutchmen held off defending section champion #3 Sa in their semifinal victory.
