New animal shelter established at Albany County Jail

A new animal shelter was established at the Albany County Jail in Colonie, as reported by WTEN News10. The facility will help increase the capacity of the Mohawk Hudson Humane Society and offer job opportunities for individuals facing homelessness, according to Albany County Sheriff Craig Apple.
