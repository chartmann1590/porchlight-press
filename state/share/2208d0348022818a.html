New animal shelter opens at the Albany County Jail

A new animal shelter has opened at the Albany County Jail in Colonie. According to Albany County Sheriff Craig Apple, the shelter will expand capacity for the Mohawk Hudson Humane Society and give employment opportunities to people experiencing homelessness.
