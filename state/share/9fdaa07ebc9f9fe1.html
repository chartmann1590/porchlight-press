Colonie Police Use Drone to Track Burglary Suspect in Woods

Colonie Police used a drone to locate and arrest a burglary suspect hiding in woods

Colonie Police employed a drone to assist officers in locating a burglary suspect who had concealed himself in a wooded area. According to WNYT, drone pilots aided patrol officers after the suspect entered the woods. The drone team detected the thermal signature of a person lying in the wood.
