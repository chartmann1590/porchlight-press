Drone Aids in Arrest of Burglary Suspect in Woods

Drone technology was used to locate a burglary suspect in wooded areas.

Colonie Police utilized a drone to assist in locating a burglary suspect who had concealed themselves in a wooded area, as reported by WNYT. The drone team detected the thermal signature of an individual lying in the woods, resulting in the suspect's arrest. WTEN News10 noted that a burglary suspect is in custody due to the use of drone and thermal technology.
