Watchdog says NY taxpayers pay $68,538 per film job

Watchdog groups Reinvent Albany and the Citizens Budget Commission criticized New York's $1.2 billion film and television tax credit program, citing studies that indicate the subsidies result in a significant net loss for taxpayers rather than providing a positive return on investment.
