BirthNet NY Aims to Address Maternal Care Gaps

BirthNet New York seeks to improve access to midwives in New York to address maternal care gaps.

BirthNet New York, cofounded by Tisha Graham, is working to enhance access to midwives in New York, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The initiative follows a March of Dimes report indicating one in three U.S. counties lacks birthing facilities or obstetric clinics. The report highlights the need for greater access to midwives in the region.
