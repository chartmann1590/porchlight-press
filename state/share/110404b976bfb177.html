Lithium-ion battery pack causes house fire in Menands

Fire department links fire to lithium-ion battery pack

A lithium-ion battery pack linked to a Dyson cordless vacuum cleaner caused a structure fire in Menands, according to WTEN News10. The fire occurred Monday evening in the Menands Fire District. West Albany Fire Department reported the incident. Menands firefighters have issued warnings about lithium-ion battery fires following two recent emergencies, as noted by WNYT NewsChannel 13.
