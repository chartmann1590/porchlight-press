Cats rescued from a trailer in Glenville are diagnosed with distemper

A rescue group said many cats taken from a trailer in Glenville are sick with a severe illness, according to WTEN News10. Some of the 39 cats pulled from a mobile home in Glenville are dying after tests confirmed feline panleukopenia, WNYT NewsChannel 13 reported. The organization placed the cats in foster homes after retrieving them from the home at the end of October.
