Berkshire County Banks Approved to Merge

Two banks in Berkshire County receive approval to combine into a single entity.

The Pittsfield Cooperative Bank and the Adams Community Bank have been approved to merge, forming Trailmark Bank. The merger was announced by WTEN News10 ABC, which reported the approval on Tuesday. Both institutions are based in Berkshire County, part of the Capital Region in New York.
