Indian Ladder Farms to host two-day craft brew festival

On Oct. 17 and 18, the Indian Ladder Farms Cidery and Brewery will be celebrating all things craft brewing with their Fresh Hop Festival. According to the farm, the event will take place from noon to 5 p.m. in the farm's Pizza Garden.
