New York sues HUD over new housing assistance rules

New York Attorney General Letitia James and a coalition of states filed a lawsuit against the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development and Secretary Scott Turner to block a rule change that requires strict citizenship verification for federal housing assistance.
