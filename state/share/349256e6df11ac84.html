GlobalFoundries to operate two new chip factories in Malta

GlobalFoundries (GF) announced Wednesday that it was selected to operate two new semiconductor facilities (fabs) in Malta. The expansion comes as part of a United States-Japan initiative providing up to $21 billion in funding.
