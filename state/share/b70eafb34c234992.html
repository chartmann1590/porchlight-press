550 Pounds of Illegal Vapes Seized in Rensselaer County

Authorities in Rensselaer County seized over 550 pounds of illegal vapes during business inspections.

The Rensselaer County Sheriff's Office reported findings from vape compliance checks at seven businesses in the county on October 29. Over 550 pounds of illicit flavored vapes were recovered from two establishments, as detailed by WTEN News10.
