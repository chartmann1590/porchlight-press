Crews rescue fisherman near Peebles Island in Cohoes

Crews conducted a water rescue in Cohoes on Wednesday afternoon. A fisherman was reportedly in distress in the area of the Mohawk River near Peebles Island at about 2:47 p.m., according to New York State Police.
