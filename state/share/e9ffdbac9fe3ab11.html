Union men's hockey seeking to retain Capital Skates Trophy

The two major pieces of hardware up for grabs each year between the Union and RPI men's hockey teams — the Mayor's Cup and the Capital Skates Trophy — both currently reside in Schenectady. Union's grip on the latter will be put to the test this weekend.
