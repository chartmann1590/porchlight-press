Thousands trick-or-treat at the Palace Theatre

ALBANY, N.Y. (NEWS10) -- Happy Halloween! On Saturday evening, thousands of people lined up in costumes for the Palace Theatre’s 5th annual Safe Trick-or-Treat event. With costumes from a police officer to a killer clown, over 4,000 people lined up at the Palace Theatre to celebrate Halloween. Kids
