Revolutionary War soldiers laid to rest at Saratoga National Cemetery

Unidentified Continental Army soldiers honored at Saratoga National Cemetery in Saratoga County

Unidentified Continental Army soldiers were laid to rest at Gerald B.H. Solomon Saratoga National Cemetery in Saratoga County, according to WTEN News10. The ceremony occurred 250 years after a Revolutionary War battle on Lake Champlain. The event included military funeral honors, as reported by WTEN News10. The remains were interred 250 years after the Battle of Valcour Island, as noted in the WAMC Northeast Public Radio report.
