2026 Coats for Kids Drive Launched in Troy

Local dry cleaners join drive to collect coats for children

The 2026 Coats for Kids Drive kicked off in Troy, New York, with local dry cleaners participating. Rainbow Cleaner's in Troy is one of the businesses accepting donations of gently used coats. The drive runs until November 6th and is supported by NEWS10 and Capital Region Honda dealerships. The National Cleaners Association is also involved in the initiative.
