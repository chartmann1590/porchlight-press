Smith College delays childcare center closure

Smith College has delayed the closure of its childcare center after parent and teacher pushback.

Smith College has postponed the closure of its childcare center following concerns from parents and educators. According to a statement from the college, Smith College President Sarah Willie-LeBreton indicated the facility will remain open through summer 2029.
