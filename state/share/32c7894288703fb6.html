Woman sentenced for transporting drugs to sell in Schenectady

A woman was sentenced to prison on September 22 after being accused of trying to drive from Syracuse to Schenectady to sell illegal drugs. 28-year-old Lesly Kantarevic received five years in prison and two years of post-release supervision.
