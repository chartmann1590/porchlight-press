Albany Street Fair Draws Hundreds

Albany's Upper Madison Street Fair attracts hundreds of visitors annually.

The Upper Madison Street Fair in Albany has drawn hundreds of visitors annually, according to WTEN News10 ABC. The event, which has been held for 15 years, features over 100 vendors offering art, music, and food. Organizer Anne Savage describes the fair as an opportunity to embrace and learn about Albany's artistic history.
