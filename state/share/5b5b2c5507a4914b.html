New York State Wildlife Plan Includes Plants, Excludes Apex Predators

New York State's updated Wildlife Action Plan includes plants for the first time but excludes apex predators.

The updated New York State Wildlife Action Plan has been released, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The plan, which outlines guidelines for conserving and managing the state's Species of Greatest Conservation Need, now includes plants for the first time but omits carnivores, as reported in the source material.
