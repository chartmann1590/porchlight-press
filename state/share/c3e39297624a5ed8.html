New company plans Asian supermarket on former National Lead site in Colonie

A new company has planned to build an Asian supermarket on land previously used by National Lead Industries in Colonie.

Asia Center Mall LLC has planned to construct an Asian supermarket on the former National Lead site in Colonie, according to WTEN News10. The site, which underwent a 34-year cleanup for contamination, is now set for potential final approval soon. The company had previously announced plans for the supermarket in 2023.
