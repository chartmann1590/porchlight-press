What could replace former National Lead site in Colonie

Following a roughly 34-year-long clean up of the contamination caused by National Lead Industries in Colonie, a new company is ready to build on the site. The Asia Center Mall LLC made plans to put an Asian Supermarket there in 2023, and now the town said final approval could come soon.
