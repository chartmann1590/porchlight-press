Galway Volleyball Team Extends 100-Victory Streak

Galway's volleyball team extended its 100-victory streak in the WAC.

Galway's girls volleyball team extended its 100-victory streak in the Western Athletic Association (WAC) with a three-set win against Fonda-Fultonville, according to WTEN News10. The team's 100th consecutive league victory was achieved last month, and the streak has now reached 110, with the Class C powerhouse set to continue into 2027.
