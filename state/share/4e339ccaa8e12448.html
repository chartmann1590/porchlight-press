Andy Taylor, guitarist of new wave icons Duran Duran, dies at 65

Taylor revealed his battle with Stage 4 metastatic prostate cancer in 2022. He was the guitarist of the 1980s English band Duran Duran, whose pop hits included "Hungry Like the Wolf" and "Rio".
