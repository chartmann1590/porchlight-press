Hundreds gather for a vigil for the Rizzo family

On the field where Abigail Rizzo cheered, the Troy community gathered for her family. Hundreds of people filled the Brunswick Sports Complex on Sunday night to remember Abigail, her mother, Alicia, and her father, Dominick Rizzo Jr., who tragically passed away after a house fire one week ago.
