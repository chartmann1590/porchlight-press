Plug-in solar bill could prevent power shortfalls in New York

Two Democratic New York state legislators are urging Governor Kathy Hochul to sign the Solar Up Now NY Act to address a projected statewide electricity shortfall beginning in 2033 by allowing residents to use plug-in solar devices.
