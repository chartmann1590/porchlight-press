Rare Declaration of Independence print on display at SUNY Purchase

250 years ago, just weeks before the Continental Army was run out of the New York City metro area, colonists heard the words of the Declaration of Independence. As America marks the document's anniversary, 1 of 5 copies read to New York residents is on display at SUNY Purchase.
