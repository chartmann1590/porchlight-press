NWS issues fire risk warning for Hudson Valley

The National Weather Service issued a special weather statement on October 30, 2026, warning of an elevated fire risk in the Hudson Valley. The statement highlights dry fuels, gusty northwest winds, and low humidity as factors contributing to potential fire spread. The warning covers Warren, Washington, Saratoga, Schenectady, Albany, Rensselaer, Greene, Columbia, Ulster, and Dutchess counties. The NWS advises caution with potential ignition sources and proper disposal of cigarette butts.
