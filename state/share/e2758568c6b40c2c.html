New learning center for neurodivergent youth opens in Latham

Bring on the Spectrum (BOTS) has established a new hands-on learning center for neurodivergent youth and young adults to develop workplace skills in Latham. The facility, situated inside Launchbox at 6 Northway Lane, was inaugurated with a ribbon-cutting event on Tuesday. The center is part of BOTS Venture Lab (BVL).
