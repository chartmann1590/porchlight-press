New York State Grants for Adaptive Fashion

New York State is offering $10,000 grants to support adaptive fashion projects.

New York State has introduced $10,000 grants to aid designers, entrepreneurs, and researchers in developing clothing that is both stylish and accessible for individuals with disabilities, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC. The initiative is managed by RPI's NY Fashion Innovation Center. The program is based in New York.
