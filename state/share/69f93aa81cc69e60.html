Dems want Hochul to drop $23.9 billion nuclear subsidy plan

Following the conclusion of the public comment period for New York's proposed nuclear policy, a coalition of 36 Democratic state legislators issued an open letter to Governor Kathy Hochul opposing the plan due to concerns over rising energy costs and long construction timelines.
