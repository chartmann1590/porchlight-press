Judge sets March retrial date for Lindsay Clancy

Massachusetts judge schedules retrial for Lindsay Clancy in connection with deaths of her three children

A Massachusetts judge has ordered a retrial for Lindsay Clancy in the case involving the deaths of her three children. The trial is scheduled for late March. Superior Court Judge William Sullivan's decision came after objections from prosecutors regarding scheduling conflicts. Clancy faces charges of first-degree murder.
