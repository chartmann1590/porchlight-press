Schenectady Mayor Veto Kills Absence Limit Law

Schenectady mayor vetoed legislation to limit council member absences

WAMC Northeast Public Radio reported that Schenectady Mayor vetoed a law aimed at limiting absences for city council members. The measure would have allowed three-quarters of the council to eject members absent for over three months. The legislation followed Councilmember Carmel Patrick's stroke, which left him absent for seven months.
