Greenport Road Repairs Face Costly Challenges

Greenport residents await repairs for a road damaged by heavy rains.

Residents of Greenport continue to wait for repairs following damage to Mount Merino Road caused by heavy rains in July. Highway Superintendent John Mausolf indicated the town is exploring short-term solutions for reopening part of the road. The road is expected to require significant repairs, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC.
