Albany Veteran Receives Home Repairs

Albany veteran receives new gutters and flag through 'Gutters for Good' program.

Sergeant Michael Slaughter, a 39-year veteran of the U.S. Army National Guard, received new gutters and a flag through the 'Gutters for Good' program in Albany, according to WTEN News10. Slaughter, who is 90% disabled on a fixed-income, faced challenges in completing home repairs upon his retirement.
