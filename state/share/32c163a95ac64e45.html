Fort Edward UFSD interim superintendent announces resignation

The interim superintendent of the Fort Edward Union Free School District has announced he will be resigning from his position. On Friday, Interim Superintendent of Schools Benjamin Halsey shared that he has submitted his resignation, which will go into effect at the end of the workday on December 11
