Nationwide ground stop lifted, but disruptions continue

A nationwide ground stop was ordered Friday for the second time since 9/11. With over 3,500 planes circling in the air and thousands of flights impacted. The order has since been lifted, but there are still numerous delays and concerns from passengers on what caused the disruption.
