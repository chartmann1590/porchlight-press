Claverack residents push for Route 9H safety improvements as study continues

Back in April, four people were killed in a head-on crash on Route 9H near Van Wyck Lane in Claverack. Investigators say a northbound Toyota Prius crossed the center line and hit a southbound Toyota Crown. Since then, residents have been pushing for safety improvements.
