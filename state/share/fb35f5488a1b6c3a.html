Market 32, CDTA host 'Stuff a Bus' donation drive for customers

Market 32 and the Capital District Transportation Authority (CDTA) are offering community members a way to give back this holiday season. The "Stuff a Bus" initiative kicked off Friday at Market 32 at 873 New Loudon Road in Latham.
