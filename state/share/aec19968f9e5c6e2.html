Schuylerville rolls to third straight Class C Super Bowl win

CLIFTON PARK, N.Y. (WNYT) — Junior Ernie DaCosta led the Black Horses with four touchdowns, as Schuylerville ran past Hoosick Falls/Tamarac 36-2 in the Class C Super Bowl.
