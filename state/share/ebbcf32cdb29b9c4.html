One dead after being struck by aircraft in Essex County

An investigation is underway after a person was struck and killed by an aircraft at a privately owned airfield in Essex County on Friday. According to New York State Police, the aircraft hit an individual in its path at Catalano Airfield in Wilmington at about 3:47 p.m.
