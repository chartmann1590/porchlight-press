Glens Falls football outlasts Averill Park to win third straight section title in overtime 28-21

Glens Falls football is known for scoring, but its defense ultimately decided the outcome of Saturday's Section 2 Class B championship game. The Black Bears came up with a clutch stop in overtime, holding off Averill Park to secure their third straight section title with a 28-21 win.
