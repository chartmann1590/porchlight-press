New Paltz voters reject consolidation ballot measure

A proposal to merge New Paltz town and village failed in local elections

A proposal to merge New Paltz town and village was rejected by voters Tuesday. Village residents voted against consolidation 59% to 40%, according to unofficial results. Town residents opposed the measure 64% to 35%.
