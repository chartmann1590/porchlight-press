Democrat Rogerson Unseats Tedisco in New York Legislature

New York legislature election sees Democrat Sarah Rogerson edge out GOP leader Jim Tedisco

WAMC Northeast Public Radio reports Democrat Sarah Rogerson secured a narrow lead over state Sen. Jim Tedisco in the New York Legislature election. Rogerson led Tedisco by 5,428 votes, according to the report. Unofficial election results from the New York State Board of Elections were released Tuesday night.
