Democrat Rogerson on brink of unseating Tedisco, longtime GOP leader in New York Legislature

While results from the New York State Board of Elections were unofficial Tuesday night, Democrat Sarah Rogerson led state Sen. Jim Tedisco by 5,404 votes, as of 2:32 a.m., with one election district still to report results.
