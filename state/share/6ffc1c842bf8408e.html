Saratoga Springs Youth Ballet hosts Nutcracker Tea event to raise funds for December production

Saratoga Springs Youth Ballet began the holiday season with their annual Nutcracker Tea event, as reported by WTEN News10. The event supports the full December production of The Nutcracker. Forty dancers performed scenes from the play with a read-along. Lunch and sweets were provided during the event.
