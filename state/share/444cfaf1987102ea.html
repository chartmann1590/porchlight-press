Ulster County executive signs order banning AI surveillance cameras

Ulster County Executive Jen Metzger issued an executive order restricting the use of AI surveillance technology. The order prohibits all county departments and staff from acquiring, hiring for, or using Flock cameras or similar AI-assisted surveillance systems, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC.
