Albany's LaSalle School opens youth center in Pine Hills neighborhood

On Wednesday, the LaSalle School in Albany unveiled a youth-oriented campus center in the Pine Hills neighborhood. According to the school, The Landing at LaSalle will provide a safe and supportive space for teens and young adults to gather while also giving them helpful day-to-day resources.
