Rte. 146 in Guilderland reopens after motor vehicle crash

A single motor vehicle crash occurred in Guilderland Monday evening in the area of State Route 146 and Gardner Road. According to Guilderland Police, a telephone pole came down and fell across the roadway of State Route 146.
