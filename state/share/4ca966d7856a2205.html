Pittsfield man found guilty of transporting minor for sex

A Pittsfield man accused of transporting a child between New York and Massachusetts with the intent to have sex, has been found guilty by a federal jury. According to the Massachusetts Department of Justice, 53-year-old Paul Newton-Irelan faces to 10 years to life in prison.
