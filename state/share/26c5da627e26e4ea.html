Hudson Valley Restaurant Week returns Nov. 2-16

The Hudson Valley Restaurant Week is returning this fall for two weeks. According to the "Hudson Valley Magazine," participating restaurants will be offering prix fixe menus across the Hudson Valley from Nov. 2 to 16.
