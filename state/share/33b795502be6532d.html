Advocates pushing for expanded review of data centers in NY

A handful of organizational leaders gathered outside the Empire State Plaza Wednesday afternoon, calling on Governor Kathy Hochul to slow down the process surrounding data centers. This comes after Hochul signed a data center moratorium over the summer, that advocates said is not enough.
