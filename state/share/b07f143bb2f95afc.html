Horses Rescued from Canada Slaughter Route Recover at NY Facility

Horses caught en route to slaughter in Canada recover at New York rescue center

Horses rescued from a Canada slaughter route recover at 13 Hands Equine Rescue in the Hudson Valley. Twenty-five horses caught in September en route to being slaughtered in Canada are part of the facility's recovery efforts, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The rescue center focuses on animals rescued from cruelty cases, with donkeys, goats, and zebras also included in recovery efforts.
