Nurses in Saranac Lake picket for better health benefits

New York State Nurses Association is currently negotiating a new contract with the hospital in Saranac Lake. One of the union's goals is to gain access to the Benefits Fund, which NYSNA says offers nurses "better and more affordable health benefits to nurses" than the hospital's healthcare plan.
