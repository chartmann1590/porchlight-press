Troy Council Rejects Crow Deterrence Resolution

Troy City Council rejected a proposal to use non-lethal methods for crow control.

The Troy City Council on Thursday rejected a resolution that would have allowed non-lethal methods to deter crows in the city, as reported by WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The council voted unanimously against the proposal, according to the source.
