NY Supreme Court Judge wants more of 'Cornell 7' on petition to remove AG James from case

One of the seven Cornell students accused of a 2024 gang rape has petitioned to remove New York Attorney General Letitia James as special prosecutor. A state Supreme Court judge is giving the petitioners a week to have more of the accused join the petition.
