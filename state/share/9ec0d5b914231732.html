Take home a scoop of Toll Gate Ice Cream history from estate sale

A former ice cream shop that served the Capital Region area for over 75 years is allowing community members to take a piece, or a full scoop, of its history home with them. An estate sale will be held for Toll Gate Ice Cream on Saturday and Sunday at 1569 New Scotland Road in Slingerlands.
