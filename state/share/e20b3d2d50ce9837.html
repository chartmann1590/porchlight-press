Horse Racing Fire Safety Committee Releases First Recommendations

Saratoga County officials released fire safety recommendations following a fatal fire in Saratoga Springs.

Saratoga Springs County Supervisor Sarah Burger established the Horse Racing Fire Safety and Prevention Advisory Committee after a fatal fire on June 16. The committee released their first set of recommendations focusing on structure, fire resistance, fire barriers, and fire suppression, according to WTEN News10.
