Fire in Colonie kills two, injures firefighter

A house fire in Colonie killed two people and injured a firefighter

A house fire in Colonie killed two people and injured a firefighter, according to WNYT NewsChannel 13. The fire broke out at a home on Locust Park near Sand Creek Road, and emergency crews responded at about 5:25 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 13, according to Fuller Road Fire Department Chief Michael Romano. Two people were killed in the fire, and a firefighter was injured.
