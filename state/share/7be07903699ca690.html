2 arrested in months-long Kingston hit-and-run investigation

Two people have been arrested following a two-month long investigation into a hit-and-run in Kingston that put a pedestrian into a coma. 35-year-old Justin Greico and 39-year-old Katie Potter were arrested and charged on Wednesday, the Kingston Police Department said.
