Albany County issues Code Blue alert through the weekend

Albany County has issued a Code Blue alert through the weekend due to cold temperatures.

Albany County has announced a Code Blue alert beginning Thursday night through Sunday, according to WTEN News10. The alert was issued by the Homeless and Travelers Aid Society (HATAS) due to expected 'real feel' temperatures reaching the low 30s and upper 20s. The alert is in effect through the weekend.
