Albany bishop hopeful he'll meet 'healthy pope' next year in wake of Leo's cancer diagnosis

Pope Leo XIV's doctors say his recovery is proceeding as planned following an operation to remove a cancerous mass from his right lung. Bishop Mark O'Connell of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Albany, who says he's set to meet the pope this spring, discussed the latest with WAMC's Andrew Pugliese.
