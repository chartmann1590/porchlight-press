Schenectady County Restaurant Week returning in 2027

Discover Schenectady, a non-profit organization dedicated to travel and tourism, has announced the 2027 dates for the Schenectady County Restaurant Week. From March 1 to March 7, restaurants around the county will be offering prix-fixe menus.
