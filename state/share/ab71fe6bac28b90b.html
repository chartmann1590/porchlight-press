Second day of testimony in Samantha Humphrey murder trial

Thursday marked the second day of testimony in the trial of Raymond Wright Jr., the man accused of killing his on-again-off-again girlfriend, 14-year-old Samantha Humphrey. Humphrey disappeared in November 2022, and her body was found in the Mohawk River months later.
