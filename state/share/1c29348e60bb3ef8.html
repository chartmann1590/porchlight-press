Albany Brewery to Close After Nine Years

Albany brewery Fort Orange Brewing will close on December 19 after 9 years in business.

Fort Orange Brewing announced it will close on December 19, according to WTEN News10. The brewery, which has been in business for nearly a decade, will end operations after growing from a dream in a garage, as reported by WNYT NewsChannel 13.
