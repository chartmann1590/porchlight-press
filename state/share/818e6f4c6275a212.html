Mental health crisis response reforms under discussion in Berkshires

Reforms for mental health crisis response are being considered in Berkshire County after two incidents involving young men of color shot by police.

Reforms for mental health crisis response are being considered in Berkshire County after two incidents involving young men of color shot by police, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. In 2022 and again this January, young men of color were fatally shot by police during mental health crises. Law enforcement in Pittsfield and Hinsdale were legally exonerated in both cases. Calls for reform have since increased.
