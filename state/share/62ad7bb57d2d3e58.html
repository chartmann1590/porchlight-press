Frost advisory issued for Capital Region

The National Weather Service issued a frost advisory for the Capital Region, affecting areas in eastern New York and Litchfield County, Connecticut. The advisory is in effect until 8 AM EDT on October 7. Frost formation is expected between 33 and 36 degrees, with potential impacts on sensitive outdoor vegetation. The advisory warns that uncovered plants may be damaged or killed.
