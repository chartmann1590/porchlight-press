Troy mayor vetoes regulations on automated license plate readers

Troy Mayor Carmella Mantello has vetoed a law passed by the Troy City Council on October 1 that aims to regulate the city's Flock Safety automated license plate readers. Mantello announced her veto of Local Law 3 on Thursday evening.
