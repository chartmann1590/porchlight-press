Authorities have identified the remains found in East Greenbush as belonging to a missing man.

Authorities have identified the remains found in East Greenbush as belonging to Joseph Viola. Viola went missing in April 2024, and his remains were identified using dental records, according to WAMC Northeast Public Radio. The skeletal remains were found in East Greenbush on Nov. 9, and authorities have identified them, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC.
