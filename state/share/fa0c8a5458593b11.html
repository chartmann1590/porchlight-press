Stewart's recalls two half-gallon ice cream flavors

Stewart's Shops is recalling two half-gallon ice cream flavors that were sold at all Stewart's locations between July 17 and October 2. Stewart's said the Heavenly Medley and Vanilla-Chocolate-Strawberry half-gallons could potentially have metal in them.
