UAlbany women's basketball fends off Bucknell

After a pair of grueling road tests against power four opponents, the UAlbany women's basketball team returned to the Broadview Center Wednesday and returned to its winning ways. Madison Roman led the Great Danes with a double-double in a 65-59 triumph over Bucknell.
