Voorheesville Quiet Zone project underway

VOORHEESVILLE, N.Y. (NEWS10) -- A long-time project in Voorheesville to build "Quiet Zones" at a noisy freight train crossing is moving forward. The area of concern is Voorheesville Avenue and South Main Street. The Shreibers have been residents of Voorheesville since the early 1970s. Joyce Shreiber
