One dead, another injured in Montgomery County head-on crash

One person has died and another was injured in a head-on collision in Montgomery County on Friday. The two-vehicle accident occurred at about 12:25 p.m. on State Highway 5S in the Town of Florida, according to the Montgomery County Sheriff's Office.
