Shenendehowa, Stillwater girls soccer finish as runners-up in respective state tournaments

A pair of stellar seasons for the Shenendehowa and Stillwater girls soccer teams culminated in state championship game appearances. Neither team was able to pull out a victory in Sunday's finals at Dryden High School, but both squads could proudly walk away with well-deserved state finalist plaques.
