Veteran's Home Burned in Fire Caused by Lithium-Ion Battery

Fire caused by lithium-ion battery leads to veteran's relocation

Michael Andrews, a veteran living in East Nassau, lost his home to a fire caused by a lithium-ion battery used in his woodworking business. The fire occurred over the winter, leading Andrews to move into a 40-foot camper on his property. The battery was left plugged in overnight, according to the source.
