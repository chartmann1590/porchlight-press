NWS Issues Fire Risk Warning for Hudson Valley

NWS Albany NY issues fire risk warning for Hudson Valley

The National Weather Service issued a special weather statement warning of an elevated fire risk in the Hudson Valley. The statement, issued October 29 at 1:36PM EDT, highlights dry fuels, gusty northwest winds up to 25 to 35 mph, and low relative humidity values. The warning covers Warren, Washington, Saratoga, Schenectady, Albany, Rensselaer, Greene, Columbia, Ulster, and Dutchess counties. The lowest relative humidity and strongest wind gusts are expected during the late morning to mid afternoon hours on Friday. Use extra caution with potential ignition sources.
