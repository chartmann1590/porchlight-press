New York Labor Department to shut down unemployment services

New York State Department of Labor will halt unemployment insurance services for nine to 12 days starting November 2

The New York State Department of Labor will temporarily suspend unemployment insurance services for nine to 12 days beginning November 2. The shutdown is part of a system upgrade to replace its 50-year-old processing system, as reported by WTEN News10 ABC.
