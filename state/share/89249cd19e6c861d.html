Downtown Schenectady Grocery to Open in 2027

New co-op grocery store set to open after 13 years of waiting

A new co-op grocery store is set to open in downtown Schenectady in 2027, following 13 years of anticipation. The Electric City Community Grocery will address a long-standing issue of limited access to reliable groceries in the area, where residents have lived in a "food desert" for over a decade. Groundbreaking occurred on Friday morning, according to WTEN News10. WAMC Northeast Public Radio reported that the store is expected to open this spring after the groundbreaking event.
