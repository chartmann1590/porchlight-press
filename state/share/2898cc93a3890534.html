Saratoga Springs Restaurant Closes After 38 Years

A Saratoga Springs dining establishment is ending its 38-year tenure.

The Wheatfields Restaurant & Bar, situated at 440 Broadway in downtown Saratoga Springs, will shut its doors. Tim and Colleen Holmes revealed the decision through a Facebook post on Wednesday.
