Cruisin' Car Show returning to Tannersville

The 14th annual Cruisin' Car Show on the Mountaintop is set to return to the painted village in the sky on Sunday. The fall show will feature a variety of classic and vintage cars and trucks throughout Main Street in Tannersville from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
