Ex-Santa impersonator turns himself in on attempted rape charges in Essex Co.

A former Santa Claus impersonator who had a warrant out for his arrest turned himself in Thursday afternoon, according to Ticonderoga police. 75-year-old Clarence Russell, of Paul Smiths failed to show up to his scheduled arraignment on Wednesday.
