Nelson Avenue Ext. closure in Saratoga due to bridge work

State Department of Transportation announces bridge closure in Saratoga Springs

The New York State Department of Transportation has confirmed that the bridge on Nelson Avenue Extension in Saratoga Springs will remain closed for an extended period. The closure is part of ongoing work on the bridge that carries over the Northway, as reported by WTEN News10. Motorists are advised that the bridge will be closed until the fall of 2027, according to WNYT NewsChannel 13.
