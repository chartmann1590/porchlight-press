DEC plans to mark 12 formerly trailless peaks in the Catskills

The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (DEC) has finalized plans to mark 12 formerly trailless peaks in the Catskill Mountains. According to the DEC, the plans were created to limit the amount of informal trails that have been made due to increased visitation.
