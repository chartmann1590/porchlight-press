Westchester man admits benefit fraud in Albany County

A Westchester County man pleaded guilty to benefit fraud in Albany County Court.

Danilo Pesce of Pelham acknowledged taking over $140,000 in pension benefits from his deceased neighbor, as reported by WTEN News10. Pesce was found guilty of fourth-degree grand larceny in Albany County Court. The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) disclosed the case.
