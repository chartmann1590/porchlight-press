Empire State Weekly: New York State pushing to close "Voluntary Intoxication" loophole

New York lawmakers are pushing to fix multiple shortcomings in the state's sexual assault laws, specifically the voluntary intoxication loophole, which says a victim cannot say they consented to an act if they were intoxicated by their own choice.
