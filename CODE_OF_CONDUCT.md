# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Porchlight Press to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or political views.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect differing viewpoints. This is a news project, and people will disagree about the news. Debate ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Hateful or discriminatory comments
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Using project spaces for political campaigning or advertising
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and email) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **[me@charleshartman.com](mailto:me@charleshartman.com)**. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock conversations, or block people who break this Code of Conduct, temporarily or permanently, based on how serious the problem is.
