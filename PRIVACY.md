# Privacy Policy

**Porchlight Press**
**Effective date:** September 23, 2026
**Contact:** [me@charleshartman.com](mailto:me@charleshartman.com)

This policy explains what information the Porchlight Press Android app ("the app", "we", "us") uses, what leaves your phone, who receives it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose. If anything is unclear, email us.

## The short version

- We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't know who you are.
- **We run no servers that collect your data.** The news the app downloads is a set of public files that are the same for everyone in your area.
- Your location, reading history, saved stories, searches, and settings are **stored only on your phone**.
- Translation and read-aloud **run on your phone**.
- The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Ads are the main way third parties receive information from the app, and **you choose** whether those ads can be personalized.
- Crash reports and usage statistics are **off unless you turn them on**.

## 1. Information that stays on your phone

The following is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it:

| Information | Why the app keeps it |
|---|---|
| Your chosen places (town, county, region, state, country) | To build your local paper |
| Approximate coordinates of your places, rounded to about 1 km | To find your town and your weather |
| ZIP / postal code you typed | To look up your town. The lookup happens on your phone |
| Stories you've downloaded, saved, or searched for | Offline reading, bookmarks, search |
| Language, voice, theme, text size, and notification settings | Your preferences |
| Downloaded translation and voice files | On-device translation and read-aloud |
| Downloaded PDF editions | So you can read, open, or share them |

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android Settings deletes all of this.

## 2. Location

- Location access is **optional**. You can type a ZIP/postal code or pick your town from a list instead.
- If you allow it, the app asks only for **approximate (coarse) location**, never precise GPS. It converts that to a town name on your phone and keeps coordinates rounded to about 1 km. The app never tracks your location in the background.
- The app never sends your location to us.

## 3. What leaves your phone, and who receives it

The app connects to the services below. Like any internet connection, each one can see your device's **IP address** and basic technical details (such as app version and device type) when it's contacted.

| Service | When | What it receives | Their privacy policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GitHub Pages** (hosts our news files) | Downloading editions and stories | Which public edition file is requested (for example the Schenectady, NY edition), which reveals the **town** you follow. No other personal information | [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) |
| **U.S. National Weather Service** (US places) | Weather and alerts | A location **rounded to about 0.1° (≈ 11 km / 7 miles)** | [weather.gov privacy](https://www.weather.gov/privacy) |
| **MET Norway** (places outside the US) | Weather | A location **rounded to about 0.1°** | [MET Norway privacy](https://www.met.no/en/About-us/privacy) |
| **Wikimedia Commons** | Loading some licensed photos | The image requested | [Wikimedia Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) |
| **Google AdMob** (ads) | When ads are shown | See section 4 | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) |
| **Google Firebase Remote Config** | Occasionally, to fetch app settings (for example, turning a feature off if it breaks) | A Firebase installation ID (a random app-instance ID, not your name or advertising ID) | [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| **Google Firebase Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, and Analytics** | **Only if you turn them on** | Crash reports, performance timings, and which screens are used. Never your location, what stories you read, or your searches | [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| **Google ML Kit** (translation) | When you pick a language | The language model is downloaded from Google. **Your text is translated on your phone and never sent for translation.** ML Kit may send Google limited technical and usage data about the feature (such as performance and error metrics) | [ML Kit terms & privacy](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) |
| **Google Play** | Installing the app and downloading voice packs | Handled by Google Play under your Google account | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| **News publishers' websites** | Only when **you** tap "Read original" | Whatever that website collects. It opens in your browser under that publisher's own privacy policy | Each publisher's own policy |

We don't sell your information, and there's none of yours on our side to sell.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from **Google AdMob**.

- **What AdMob may collect:** your device's **advertising ID**, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
- **Your choice:** where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK, and some US states), the app shows Google's consent screen **before any ads load**. If you don't consent to personalized ads, you see **non-personalized ads**, which still use limited data for things like fraud prevention and frequency capping. You can change your choice any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **Opting out on Android:** you can delete or reset your advertising ID in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**.
- We don't share your location, reading history, or searches with advertisers. Ads are never placed inside stories, in PDFs, in shared links, or in notifications.

## 5. Crash reports and statistics (opt-in)

Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, and Analytics are **turned off by default**. If you turn them on in onboarding or Settings, the app sends crash reports, performance timings, and screen-level usage to Google Firebase to help us fix problems. You can turn them off at any time and collection stops immediately.

## 6. Notifications

Notifications are generated **on your phone**, after the app checks for new editions and weather alerts. We don't use push-notification tokens, so no server knows you have notifications turned on.

## 7. Translation and read-aloud

- **Translation:** stories and menus are translated on your phone by Google ML Kit. Your text is never sent to a translation server.
- **Read-aloud:** voices run on your phone. Voice files come from Google Play as part of the app. What you listen to is never sent anywhere.

## 8. Sharing and "Report a problem"

- **Sharing** a story or PDF uses Android's share menu. The app sends only what you choose to share, to the app you choose.
- **Report a problem with this story** opens an email in your own email app, pre-filled with the story's ID. We receive only what you send, including your email address, and use it only to review the story and reply to you.

## 9. Children

Porchlight Press is a general news app and is **not directed at children under 13** (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has contacted us, email us and we'll delete the message.

## 10. How long information is kept

- **On your phone:** until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall. Old editions are cleared automatically after a few days; saved stories stay until you remove them.
- **Emails you send us:** kept only as long as needed to respond, then deleted within 12 months.
- **Google services** keep data according to their own policies linked above.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to its use.

- Because we don't hold personal information about you on any server, most of these rights are exercised **directly on your phone**: change your settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall.
- For data held by Google (ads and the optional Firebase features), use Google's tools, reset your advertising ID, or change your ad choice in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- For anything else, email [me@charleshartman.com](mailto:me@charleshartman.com). We'll respond within 30 days.

## 12. Security

Every connection the app makes uses encryption (HTTPS). The app doesn't display web content from news feeds as live web pages; it shows it as plain text. To report a security problem, see our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## 13. International users

The services listed above may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, Google relies on approved legal mechanisms for international transfers.

## 14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app. The full history of this policy is public in this repository.
